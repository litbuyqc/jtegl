The quick answer

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Depending on its creator, it may include categories, listed prices, sizing notes and image references. Treat it as a starting point: verify the current listing before making a purchase decision.

Before you browse

Good questions.
Direct answers.

A few clear checks can make a product discovery list much easier to use.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Depending on its creator, it may include categories, listed prices, sizing notes and image references. Always verify details on the current product page.

Is this page a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is an independent category directory and comparison guide. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS; it does not provide a downloadable sheet or an Oopbuy checkout.

How should I compare clothing and shoe finds?

Compare the exact variant, size chart, stated materials, seller information and estimated total cost. For clothing, compare garment measurements with an item you own. For shoes, check the seller’s sizing guidance.

Does a listed product price include delivery?

Do not assume that it does. Check whether domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, taxes or duties apply before deciding on a budget.

Do brand names or illustrations confirm authenticity?

No. Brand references and conceptual illustrations do not verify a product, seller or brand affiliation. Check the actual listing and any available evidence of authenticity separately.
